# Membership Levels

Membership Levels recognise continuous active membership.

They are awarded automatically.

---

## Bronze Member 🥉

Active for less than 1 year.

---

## Silver Member 🥈

Active continuously for 1–3 years.

---

## Gold Member 🥇

Active continuously for 3–5 years.

---

## Platinum Member 💎

Active continuously for more than 5 years.

---

## Rules

Membership must remain active.

If membership expires for more than 90 days, the continuous membership count resets.

---

## Display

Membership Level appears on:

- Public Profile
- Membership Card
- Membership Certificate
- Agent Dashboard
- Membership Verification Page

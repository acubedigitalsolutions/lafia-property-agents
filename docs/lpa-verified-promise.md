# LPA Verified Promise

## Overview

The LPA Verified Promise is a professional pledge made by every registered and verified member of Lafia Property Agents.

The promise reinforces trust, professionalism, honesty, and accountability in property transactions.

---

## The Promise

As a verified member of Lafia Property Agents, I pledge to:

- Conduct my business honestly and professionally.
- Provide accurate information about properties.
- Treat every client fairly and respectfully.
- Avoid fraudulent or misleading practices.
- Comply with the Lafia Property Agents Code of Conduct.
- Protect clients' personal information.
- Promote transparency in every transaction.
- Maintain the reputation of Lafia Property Agents.

I understand that violating this promise may result in suspension or permanent removal from the platform.

---

## Acceptance

Every agent must accept the LPA Verified Promise before submitting their registration.

Checkbox:

☐ I have read and agree to the LPA Verified Promise.

The registration cannot be submitted until this box is checked.

---

## Display

The LPA Verified Promise is displayed on:

- Registration Page
- Agent Dashboard
- Membership Certificate
- Public Agent Profile
- Membership Verification Page

---

## Purpose

The promise helps to:

- Build trust with clients.
- Promote ethical behaviour.
- Improve professionalism.
- Protect the reputation of the platform.
